## Experiment

### VR Rendering / HMD Diagnostic Cases

**Purpose**

Systematically isolate the source of incorrect VR rendering behavior by modifying one component at a time.

The main components under investigation were:

- HMD pose
- Camera transformation
- Stereo eye transformation
- Projection frustum
- Render aspect ratio
- Left / right eye submission
- Coordinate conventions

**Setup**

Baseline VR implementation:

- `main_vr_scene_7.py`

Diagnostic directory:

- `code/vr_teleop/cases/2026-09-17/`

Diagnostic scripts:

1. `case_01_headpose_relative_rotation.py`
2. `case_02_projection_frustum_aspect.py`
3. `case_03_symmetric_frustum_center.py`
4. `case_04_same_image_both_eyes.py`
5. `case_05_fresh_hmd_pose.py`
6. `case_06_frustum_width_from_render_aspect.py`
7. `case_07_full_6dof_head_translation.py`
8. `case_08_predicted_hmd_pose_30ms.py`
9. `case_09_static_camera_no_vr_override.py`
10. `case_10_eye_transform_translation_only.py`
11. `case_11_swap_left_right_submit.py`
12. `case_12_zero_ipd_eye_translation.py`
13. `case_13_world_up_lock_no_roll.py`
14. `case_14_flip_frustum_center_sign.py`
15. `case_15_flip_vertical_frustum_sign.py`
16. `case_16_exact_projection_aspect.py`

**Test**

The cases were designed to independently test the following hypotheses.

### HMD Pose

- Relative HMD rotation
- Fresh HMD pose
- Full 6DoF head translation
- Predicted HMD pose with approximately 30 ms prediction
- Static camera behavior without VR pose override

### Stereo Rendering

- Identical image submission to both eyes
- Eye translation only
- Left / right eye submission swap
- Zero-IPD configuration

### Projection / Frustum

- Projection frustum aspect ratio
- Symmetric frustum center
- Frustum width based on render aspect ratio
- Frustum center sign
- Vertical frustum sign
- Exact projection aspect ratio

### Camera Orientation

- World-up locking
- Roll removal / roll behavior

**Result**

A structured diagnostic suite was successfully created.

Instead of repeatedly modifying the complete VR pipeline, individual hypotheses can now be tested independently while keeping the remaining system unchanged.

The diagnostic structure makes it possible to determine whether the rendering problem originates from:

- HMD pose calculation
- Head translation
- Pose prediction
- Eye transformation
- IPD handling
- Left / right eye mapping
- Projection matrix / frustum geometry
- Aspect ratio
- Coordinate-system convention

The final root cause of the VR rendering problem was not yet confirmed.

Per-case experimental results remain to be evaluated systematically.
